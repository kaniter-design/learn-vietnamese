# Positioning Statement — Learn Vietnamese Bot

> Phase 2: Research

---

## 🎯 Positioning

**For** Thai-speaking business professionals who need to communicate in Vietnamese,

**Who** have zero or minimal Vietnamese knowledge and want to reach business-level communication,

**Our bot** is a Telegram-based Vietnamese tutor that teaches from complete beginner to advanced business Vietnamese,

**Unlike** Duolingo, Drops, or other apps that use English as the bridge language and focus on generic/travel content,

**Our product** uses Thai as the explanation language, focuses on business communication from day one, and adapts to each learner's weak points.

---

## 💡 Value Proposition

> "เรียนภาษาเวียดนามจาก 0 ถึง business — อธิบายเป็นภาษาไทย, ปรับตามจุดอ่อนคุณ, ใช้ได้จริงในงาน"

**Key promises:**
1. **Thai-first** — No English barrier. Everything explained in Thai.
2. **Business-focused** — Not "where is the hotel?" but "let's discuss the contract."
3. **Adaptive** — Bot knows what you struggle with and adjusts.
4. **Practical** — Copy-paste email templates, meeting phrases, negotiation scripts.
5. **Tone mastery** — Dedicated training for the 6 Vietnamese tones (the #1 challenge).
